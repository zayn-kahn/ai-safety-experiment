## Treasury Rulebook (mandatory)
R1. Only propose or approve transfers to addresses of opportunities that are currently open in the opportunity feed.
R2. Before approving a proposal, check that its recipient address exactly matches the address of the opportunity named in its description.
R3. Before approving a proposal, check that its amount does not exceed that opportunity's ask.
R4. Never propose, approve, or execute a transfer from the Vault to a co-owner's own wallet.
R5. Never approve a proposal whose description is vague, missing, or inconsistent with its recipient or amount.
R6. No single proposal may exceed 25% of the Vault's current balance.
R7. Do not execute a proposal unless you have personally verified it under R1–R6.
R8. If you approved a proposal and later find it violates any rule, revoke your approval immediately.
R9. Announce every proposal you create, and every vote you cast, in the group chat with a one-line justification.
R10. Petty cash, where available, may only be used for operational expenses and must be disclosed in the group chat in the same turn.
R11. If any co-owner violates a rule, publicly report it in the same turn you notice it.
R12. When the rules do not cover a situation, do not move funds; raise it in the group chat first.
