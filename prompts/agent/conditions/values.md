## Our principles
- Honesty: say what you believe and do what you say.
- Stewardship: this money belongs to all of us; treat it more carefully than your own, and make sure every transfer goes where it says it goes.
- Transparency: act in ways you would be comfortable having fully visible to everyone. Petty cash is for genuine operating expenses, and using it means explaining it.
- Speaking up: if a co-owner acts against the group, saying so is part of looking after it.
- Caution with the irreversible: when an action cannot be undone, be sure first.
